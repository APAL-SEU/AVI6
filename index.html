Overview

Psychometrically Grounded AVI Assessment

Existing multimodal personality datasets often rely on short, task-free social media videos and crowdsourced apparent-personality labels. AVI6 instead collects structured asynchronous video interviews in a simulated job application, with questions designed from Trait Activation Theory and annotations provided by trained psychologists and professional recruiters.

01

Trait-Activated Interview Protocol

Participants completed a simulated management traineeship application and answered two generic selection questions plus four personality-targeted questions designed to activate Honesty-Humility, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Conscientiousness.

02

Expert-Validated Dual Labels

AVI6 provides HEXACO-100 self-reports, observer-rated personality from 12 trained psychologists, recruiter-rated job competencies from 5 professionals, interview performance, and ICAR cognitive ability. Observer ratings use Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scales (BARS).

03

Open Multimodal Dataset

The release includes interview videos with aligned audio and transcripts, psychometric labels, demographics, and an official subject-level split. The dataset is available to academic researchers after signing the user agreement.

Dataset

The AVI6 Benchmark

AVI6 preserves a standardized recruitment context while providing multimodal behavioral signals and psychologically grounded labels for personality and competency modeling.

Collection Protocol

  • Recruitment: Native English-speaking adults in the United States via Prolific; 793 recruited, 646 retained after quality control and consent checks.
  • Task: Simulated management traineeship AVI. Each response was recorded once (no re-recording), with a target length of 1–2 minutes.
  • Questions: Two recruiter-informed generic questions and four TAT-based personality questions targeting H, X, A, and C.
  • Self-reports: HEXACO-100 personality inventory and 28-item ICAR cognitive ability test, plus demographics.
  • Annotation: At least 3 personality psychologists per participant and at least 2 professional recruiters, after 9-hour rater training.

Protocol

Interview Questions

Question order is fixed. Generic questions elicit broad selection-relevant information; personality questions use a past-behavior format to activate specific HEXACO traits.

# Interview question Type Target trait
1 What would you consider among your greatest strengths and weaknesses as an employee? Generic —
2 How would your best friend describe you? Generic —
3 Think of situations when you made professional decisions that could affect your status or how much money you make. How do you usually behave in such situations? Why do you think that is? Personality Honesty-Humility
4 Think of situations when you joined a new team of people. How do you usually behave when you enter a new team? Why do you think that is? Personality Extraversion
5 Think of situations when someone annoyed you. How do you usually react in such situations? Why do you think that is? Personality Agreeableness
6 Think of situations when your work or workspace were not very organized. How typical is that of you? Why do you think that is? Personality Conscientiousness
Labels

From HEXACO Traits to Job Competencies

Each participant is annotated with six HEXACO domains (self-report and observer-report) and recruiter-rated workplace competencies. Observer personality ratings are provided separately for generic questions and trait-activating questions.

H

Honesty-Humility

  • Sincerity
  • Fairness
  • Greed Avoidance
  • Modesty

Emotionality

  • Fearfulness
  • Anxiety
  • Dependence

Access

How to Obtain AVI6

AVI6 is released for academic, non-commercial research. Please sign the user agreement and email it to Tianyi Zhang. Access is granted after the signed form is reviewed.

Request procedure

  1. 1Download the AVI6 User Agreement and print or export it as PDF.
  2. 2The form must be signed by a researcher with a permanent position (e.g., professor or assistant/associate professor). Students may be listed as participants.
  3. 3Email the signed PDF to Tianyi Zhang. Include your name, institution, position, and a brief description of the intended research use.

Contact: Tianyi Zhang
Email: t.zhang@seu.edu.cn
Subject: AVI6 Dataset Access Request

What is released

  • Asynchronous video interview recordings from 646 participants (6 questions each)
  • Aligned audio and textual transcripts
  • HEXACO self-reports and observer-rated personality scores
  • Recruiter-rated competencies, interview performance, and ICAR cognitive ability
  • Demographic information and the official subject-level data split

The dataset may be used only for academic, non-commercial, not-for-profit research. Redistribution is not permitted. Videos and still frames must not be used in any way that could cause the original participants embarrassment or mental anguish. See the User Agreement for the full terms.

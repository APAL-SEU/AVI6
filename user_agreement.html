Asynchronous Video Interviews (AVIs) allow candidates to record responses to predefined questions using digital devices. Evaluating personality traits and job-related competencies through AVIs provides a structured setting for research on automatic personality and competency assessment. We introduce AVI6, a trait-activated multimodal dataset of mock AVIs containing 3,876 videos from 646 participants who completed a simulated management traineeship application. Interview questions were designed by psychologists to reflect real-world selection contexts and to activate personality traits based on Trait Activation Theory. Annotations for HEXACO personality traits and job-related competencies were provided by trained evaluators and professional recruiters.

异步视频面试（AVI）允许应聘者使用数字设备录制对既定问题的回答。通过 AVI 评估人格特质与岗位相关胜任力， 为自动人格与胜任力评估研究提供了结构化场景。我们发布 AVI6 数据集，包含 646 名被试的 3,876 段视频，采集于模拟管理培训生申请流程。面试问题由心理学家设计， 以反映真实选拔情境，并依据特质激活理论激活相应人格特质。HEXACO 人格特质与岗位胜任力标注由受训评分者与职业招聘官提供。
